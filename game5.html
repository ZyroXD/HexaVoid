RFS - Real Flight Simulator

RFS provides an authentic flight simulation experience where players can take control of a variety of aircraft, flying across different regions of the world with realistic physics and visuals.

RFS - Real Flight Simulator is designed for aviation enthusiasts who wish to experience realistic flight dynamics and controls. The game features a variety of aircraft, from commercial airliners to private jets, all modeled with exceptional attention to detail. Players can fly through real-world weather conditions, plan flight routes, and handle multiple aspects of flight, including navigation, fuel management, and landing. With a vast map covering multiple continents and regions, RFS offers an unparalleled aviation experience. The simulator features intuitive controls and a detailed cockpit view, allowing players to manage every aspect of the flight. With real-time multiplayer support, you can fly alongside friends or other online pilots, making RFS an immersive and realistic flight simulation experience. If you’ve always wanted to fly a plane and experience what it’s like to soar through the skies, RFS offers an engaging, detailed, and highly realistic flight simulation that’ll satisfy your aviation curiosity.
